How It Works

1

Register & Pay

Create an account, browse open tournaments, register with your in-game name and pay the entry fee via the provided payment link.

2

Get Matched

Once the tournament starts, your match is generated. You see your opponent and the Friends Mode password to find each other in DLS.

3

Play in DLS

Open DLS → Friends Mode → enter the password shown on your dashboard. You and your opponent are matched instantly.

4

Submit Screenshot

After the match, both players upload a screenshot of the result screen. Admin reviews both screenshots and confirms the score.

5

Advance & Win

Winners automatically advance to the next round. Track your progress on your dashboard until you lift the trophy!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay the entry fee?
▼
After registering, you'll be given a unique payment reference code and a payment link. Pay via UPI/GPay/PhonePe to the provided link. Include your reference code in the payment remarks. Then submit your UTR/transaction ID on your dashboard. Admin will verify and confirm your registration within a few hours.
How are match results verified?
▼
Both players must submit a screenshot of the final score screen in DLS. Admin reviews both screenshots side by side. If both match, the score is confirmed automatically. If they don't match, admin investigates and makes a final decision.
How do I receive my prize money?
▼
Prize money is sent directly to your UPI ID or bank account via GPay/PhonePe/NEFT within 24 hours of the tournament ending. Make sure your phone number is correct in your registration so we can contact you.
What happens if my opponent doesn't show up?
▼
Each match has a 15-minute deadline from the scheduled start time. If your opponent doesn't submit their screenshot within 15 minutes, they automatically lose the match and you advance to the next round.
Can I get a refund if the tournament is cancelled?
▼
Yes! If a tournament is cancelled by admin, full refunds are processed within 24 hours to all registered players via the same UPI method used for payment. Contact us on WhatsApp if you don't receive it.
Is DLS Arena free to join?
▼
Creating an account is 100% free. We also run free tournaments regularly for new players to get started. Paid tournaments have entry fees that go directly into the prize pool for winners.
